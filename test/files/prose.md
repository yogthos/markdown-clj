# Daily Moisturising Lotion

A light, fast absorbing lotion for normal to dry skin. It is made with colloidal oatmeal and leaves the skin feeling soft and comfortable for up to 24 hours, without a greasy film.

## Why you might like it

* Absorbs within a minute, so you can get dressed straight away
* Fragrance free and suitable for sensitive skin
* Clinically shown to improve the moisture level of dry skin after **one** use
* Comes in a recyclable bottle with a pump

The lotion is suitable for adults and for children over the age of three months. If you are looking after very young skin, speak to a pharmacist before you start using a new product.

## How to use

Apply to clean, dry skin once or twice a day. Use as much as you need and massage it in gently until it is fully absorbed. It works best when it is applied straight after a bath or a shower, while the skin is still slightly damp.

1. Wash and dry your hands
2. Pump a small amount into the palm of your hand
3. Smooth it over the skin in the direction of hair growth
4. Wait a minute before getting dressed

For external use only. Avoid contact with the eyes. If the lotion gets into your eyes, rinse them well with plenty of water.

## Ingredients

Aqua, Glycerin, Distearyldimonium Chloride, Isopropyl Palmitate, Paraffinum Liquidum, Cetyl Alcohol, Dimethicone, Avena Sativa Kernel Flour, Allantoin, Paraffin, Cera Microcristallina, Stearyl Alcohol, Myristyl Alcohol, Isopropyl Alcohol, Sodium Chloride, Benzyl Alcohol.

Ingredients can change from time to time, so always check the label on the product you receive.

## Warnings

Do not use the lotion if you are allergic to any of the ingredients. Stop using it and ask a doctor or a pharmacist for advice if your skin becomes red, itchy or swollen. Keep out of the sight and reach of children.

*Store below 25°C, away from direct sunlight.* The bottle can be used for 12 months after it has been opened.

## Questions

**Can I use it on my face?** Yes, although a product made for the face may suit you better if your skin is oily.

**Is it tested on animals?** No. You can read more in the [manufacturer's policy](https://example.com/policy).

**How long does a bottle last?** A 300ml bottle lasts around six weeks when it is used once a day on the arms and legs.
